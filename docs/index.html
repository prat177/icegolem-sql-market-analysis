Founder decisions generated by the analysis

Method note: This dashboard summarizes directional founder research. The sample is small (n=25), non-probability based, and uses self-reported purchase intent rather than observed buying behavior. It should be treated as hypothesis-generating evidence, not a representative market estimate.
